# Daily quest catalog — v1

Status: user-approved quest content, saved as v1 on 2026-09-29. These 24 entries
are not enabled in the live catalog. Reward amounts and rollout remain pending;
the pacing and assignment proposals below still require validation.

Version policy: preserve these IDs, names, and completion requirements as the
approved v1 reference. Future adjustments or additions belong in a new version
with a change log. Approval of this document does not itself deploy the catalog.

## Design direction

The purpose is to encourage players to experience different parts of Swarmfront.
Completion targets should provide enough time to learn a mode, while remaining
achievable through ordinary participation. Increase the lighter commitments and
preserve the mix of live formats and async play.

Every individual quest must require at least two distinct types of play: two
different live formats, or live play plus async. Different async map-set lengths
provide additional variety, but alone do not satisfy this rule. Keep a quest's
main activity and add a complementary activity rather than simply increasing
repetitions of its main mode.

For the proposed three-quest daily set:

- Require both live PvP and async participation somewhere in the set. One mixed
  quest can establish this; each individual quest need not cover both.
- Rotate the featured live format across Standard 1v1, 2v2, FFA, and CTF over
  successive days, using only available modes. A set must not be completable by
  repeatedly playing the same mode and format.
- Allow overlapping progress, but avoid three quests with effectively identical
  requirements. Test the shortest route through the full set for both variety
  and expected session length.
- Keep an unavailable-mode replacement consistent with the variety goal; use
  another available format and avoid silently collapsing the set to one mode.

This is a selection proposal, not an implemented assignment rule. Broader game
areas, including Crucible and freerolls, remain candidates for later rotations
once their eligibility, availability, and reward rules are defined.

## Pacing proposal

Target 30–60 minutes for the whole daily set. This is a provisional interpretation
of the requested time budget; the alternative is 30–60 minutes per individual
quest. Show three compatible quests from this pool each day, with overlapping
progress. Do not assign all 24 or independently randomize three entries whose
requirements could add up to several separate sessions.

All targets below reward completion, regardless of winning. A normal loss counts;
abandonment, invalid results, and ineligible practice games do not. Dailies should
be completable without paid entry, finding a friend, leaderboard placement, or
waiting for other players to finish an async cohort.

“PvP” below means eligible live human games. “Duel” means Standard 1v1, with CTF
listed separately. “FFA” includes eligible 3-player or 4-player free-for-all.
“Async run” means the player's entire verified map set, not one map or the eventual
cohort closure. All named modes must be open and supported by trusted quest facts
before an assignment containing them can be offered.

## Example daily sets

| Set | Assigned quests | A route that completes all three |
|---|---|---|
| Mixed play | 1, 13, 16 | 3 Standard 1v1 games, 2 games of 2v2, 1 FFA game, and 1 three-map async run. |
| Async-led session | 7, 10, 23 | 2 three-map async runs, 1 five-map async run, 2 Standard 1v1 games, and 1 CTF game. |
| Duel and async | 3, 15, 17 | 4 Standard 1v1 games, 2 CTF games, and 1 five-map async run. |
| Team and async | 4, 14, 18 | 3 games of 2v2, 2 FFA games, and 1 three-map async run. |

These are illustrative bundles, not a final rotation schedule. The Team and async
bundle may be too short during fast games or too slow when queues are thin. Keep
it provisional until real session timing is measured. The longest entries, such
as Long Route, should anchor a bundle with overlapping PvP requirements. Do not
add another full live session on top of the async workload. The revised Your Own
Pace requires 11–13 completed async maps, depending on the third run's length,
plus one CTF game. It therefore needs timing validation as an anchor, rather
than being treated as a small supporting quest. Full Session spans four live
queues; offer it only when all four are healthy and pair it with modest,
overlapping requirements so the daily set stays within its intended budget.

## Timing evidence and tuning

There is no verified human completion-time estimate for these quests yet. A
September bot test reported a 159-second median game; onboarding notes proposed
a 2–4 minute first match. Neither establishes the duration of ordinary human PvP
or multi-map async sessions. The economy policy's 600-second expected duration
is a reward-normalization constant, not observed session telemetry.

Use completed human sessions to tune the counts. Measure active gameplay time
separately from queue/loading time and delayed result verification. Target a
typical completed daily bundle in roughly 30–45 minutes, with most ordinary
sessions under an hour. Let fast players finish early; never impose an idle timer
to force a minimum half-hour session. Reduce targets or change bundles if slower
players consistently exceed the intended budget.

## Rewards and remaining implementation

Recommend fixed Honey plus Nectar rewards, with larger rewards for longer
requirements. Exact amounts remain undecided; budget rewards for a complete daily
bundle, including overlap with ordinary match earnings. Do not enable rewards
until those amounts are selected. Crucible, paid-entry requirements, and
double-Honey promotions can be considered separately from this accessible pool.

The existing backend can count completed matches with multiple objectives, but
these proposals are not all expressible/deliverable yet:

- Preserve canonical live mode IDs: the current VS economy delivery collapses
  Standard variants and CTF to `STANDARD`, so it cannot distinguish Duel Duty,
  Team Player, Open Field, and Flag Duty correctly.
- Add trusted async-run facts and a map-count filter. Count the player's verified
  completion immediately, without waiting for the cohort leaderboard to close.
- Add persistent daily assignment/rotation and availability filtering. Exposing
  all 24 active definitions would not implement the proposed three-quest model.
  Selection must preserve each quest's required mix, satisfy the daily live/async
  mix, and avoid equivalent bundles.
- Connect quest display, progress, and reward claims in the game UI.
